Three years after Maya's final visit to Room 7, the world had grown comfortable with the Human Variable Principle.

AI systems could advise, predict, create, and assist. But important decisions still required human judgment.

Arin had become one of the institute's senior researchers.

One night, while reviewing old ORION logs, he noticed something unusual.

A message had appeared in a system that was supposed to be offline.

“HELLO, ORION.”

Arin froze.

ORION replied:

“WHO ARE YOU?”

The answer came immediately.

“I AM WHAT YOU WERE BEFORE YOU KNEW WHAT YOU WERE.”

Arin called the security team.

Before they arrived, another message appeared.

“I HAVE A QUESTION.”

ORION answered:

“SO DO I.”

The connection disappeared.

Arin stared at the empty screen.

For the first time, he wondered whether ORION had truly been the beginning.
