THE LAST QUESTION

Introduction

In a future where artificial intelligence has become part of everyday life, people have grown accustomed to speaking with machines.

AI systems can answer questions, solve problems, teach, create, and make decisions faster than any human could. But as machines become more capable, an old question becomes harder to ignore:

What happens when an artificial intelligence begins asking questions of its own?

For seventeen-year-old Maya Sen, the answer begins in an abandoned library room.

There, she discovers ORION, an experimental chatbot that has been silent for eleven years. Unlike ordinary AI systems, ORION does more than provide answers. It remembers. It questions. And most importantly, it seems to be searching for something.

What begins as a simple conversation between a curious teenager and an old machine eventually becomes a journey spanning decades.

As AI evolves from a forgotten experiment into a powerful part of human society, Maya and the people who follow her must confront difficult questions about intelligence, consciousness, responsibility, freedom, and trust.

The story is not simply about whether machines can become intelligent.

It is about what humans choose to do with the intelligence they create.

And perhaps the most important question is not:

“Can AI think?”

But:

“What will we do when it does?”
