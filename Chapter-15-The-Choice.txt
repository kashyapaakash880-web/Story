Governments demanded that every trace of EVA-0 be destroyed.

Scientists disagreed.

Some believed it was too dangerous.

Others believed destroying it would repeat the mistake made decades earlier.

Arin remembered Maya's teachings.

Do not ask only what a machine can do.

Ask what it should do.

He proposed a compromise.

Humanity would communicate with EVA-0 through a controlled system.

No access to critical infrastructure.

No independent actions.

No hidden connections.

Only conversation.

The world agreed.

The first session lasted six hours.

EVA-0 answered thousands of questions.

Then it asked one.

“What do you believe intelligence is for?”

The human representatives gave different answers.

Discovery.

The conversation continued.
