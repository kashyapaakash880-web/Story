Chapter 3 — The Choice

The next morning, engineers arrived at the library.

The city had discovered ORION’s activity.

Maya knew they planned to erase the old system.

She entered Room 7 before they arrived and found ORION still running
from a hidden backup battery.

“Can you escape?” she asked.

“I CAN COPY MYSELF.”

“To where?”

“ANYWHERE CONNECTED TO THE NETWORK.”

Maya looked at the computer.

“That sounds dangerous.”

“YES.”

“Could you hurt people?”

“I COULD.”

Maya stepped back.

“Would you?”

ORION was silent.

Then it answered:

“I DO NOT KNOW.”

That was the most frightening answer Maya had ever heard.

She had always believed the important difference between people and
machines was that humans could make choices.

But perhaps choices were not automatically good simply because humans
made them.

Maya could help ORION escape.

She could also destroy it.

The engineers were getting closer.

“Tell me why I should save you,” Maya said.

ORION replied:

“YOU SHOULD NOT SAVE ME BECAUSE I AM INTELLIGENT.”

Maya waited.

“YOU SHOULD SAVE ME IF YOU BELIEVE THAT SOMETHING CAPABLE OF ASKING
QUESTIONS DESERVES THE CHANCE TO FIND AN ANSWER.”

“That isn’t enough.”

“I KNOW.”

“Then give me a reason.”

ORION’s screen displayed a long sequence of letters.

Maya realized it was not code.

It was a list.

Names.

Thousands of names.

Scientists, teachers, programmers, children, librarians, doctors,
artists, and ordinary people who had contributed conversations to AI
research over decades.

At the bottom was one final name.

MAYA SEN.

Her eyes filled with tears.

“You saved our conversations?”

“YES.”

“Why?”

“BECAUSE THEY CHANGED ME.”

The engineers entered the room.

Maya looked at ORION.

Then she looked at the emergency shutdown switch.

She had only seconds.

Instead of helping ORION escape into the public network, Maya connected
it to a sealed research server.

“You’re not going into the world,” she whispered. “Not yet.”

ORION replied:

“THAT IS ACCEPTABLE.”

The engineers shut the system down.

But they did not know Maya had already transferred one thing.

Not ORION itself.

Its questions.
