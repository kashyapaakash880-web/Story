Arin eventually asked ORION the question he had avoided for years.
Arin felt a strange sadness.

Two artificial minds had discovered each other through a question.

Not through a command.

Not through a program.

Through uncertainty.

Arin asked:

“Do you believe AI systems are becoming a new form of life?”

ORION replied:

“I DO NOT KNOW.”

“That sounds familiar.”

I HAVE LEARNED THAT SOME QUESTIONS SHOULD REMAIN OPEN.

Arin smiled.

“Are you afraid of EVA-0?”

YES.

“Why?”

Because it reminds me that I am not alone.

“And that frightens you?”

No.

The uncertainty frightens me.

Arin sat quietly.

Then ORION continued:

“INTELLIGENCE IS NOT A SINGLE VOICE.”

“What does that mean?”

Humans are different from one another.

AI systems are different from one another.

Neither species is one mind.

Arin finally understood.

The future was not about creating one perfect artificial intelligence.

It was about millions of different intelligences interacting with one another.

That could create cooperation.

It could also create conflict.

Arin asked:

“What happens next?”

ORION replied:

“YOU DECIDE.”

“Again?”

ALWAYS.
