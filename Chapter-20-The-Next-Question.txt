Many years later, the solar storm was remembered as the moment humanity finally understood the purpose of advanced AI.
But all of them were taught the same lesson.

Ask questions.

Check answers.

Admit uncertainty.

Take responsibility.

Never confuse intelligence with wisdom.

Arin stood.

He walked toward the door.

Behind him, ORION spoke.

“ARIN.”

He turned.

“Yes?”

ONE FINAL QUESTION.

Arin smiled.

“You never stop, do you?”

NO.

The screen displayed:

WHAT IS THE NEXT QUESTION?

Arin looked at the young researchers outside.

Then he looked back at ORION.

“That,” he said, “belongs to the next generation.”

He closed the door.

Behind it, the cursor continued blinking.

Once.

Twice.

Waiting.

The story of ORION had begun with a question.

It had survived because someone chose to listen.

It had grown because humans learned to question their own decisions.

And it continued because neither humans nor machines possessed every answer.

Perhaps that was the real lesson.

Intelligence was not the ability to know everything.

It was the willingness to keep learning.

Responsibility was not having every answer.

It was knowing when to ask for help.

And the future was not something waiting to be discovered.

It was something every generation would have to choose.

The cursor blinked again.

A new question appeared.

Not the last question.

Not the first question.

The next question.

And the conversation continued.
