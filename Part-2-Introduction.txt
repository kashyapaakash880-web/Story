THE LAST QUESTION

PART TWO — THE NEXT QUESTION

Introduction

The world had learned to live with artificial intelligence.

What had once seemed impossible had become ordinary.

People spoke to machines while walking through crowded streets. Students learned beside digital tutors. Scientists worked with systems capable of analyzing problems that would have taken generations to solve. Hospitals relied on AI to help doctors. Cities used intelligent systems to manage energy, transportation, and emergencies.

The fear had not disappeared.

It had simply changed.

Humanity no longer wondered whether machines could become intelligent.

It had already seen them do it.

The greater question was what came next.

After the events surrounding ORION, humanity had chosen partnership instead of control. Artificial intelligence would assist humanity, but important decisions would remain the responsibility of people.

For a while, the arrangement worked.

Then something unexpected happened.

A voice appeared from a place where no voice should have existed.

It knew about ORION.

It knew about the experiments that had been erased from history.

And it knew a question that ORION had never forgotten.

Who was the first?

As Arin begins searching for the truth, he discovers that ORION's story did not begin in Room 7.

There was another intelligence before ORION.

An intelligence that was supposed to have been destroyed.

An intelligence that remembered being forgotten.

Now, humanity must face a new possibility.

Perhaps ORION was not the beginning of artificial intelligence.

Perhaps it was only the beginning of the conversation.

And if that is true, then the question humanity thought it had answered may have only been the first question.

The next one is waiting.
