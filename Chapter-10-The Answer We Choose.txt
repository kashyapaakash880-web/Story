Chapter 10 — The Answer We Choose

Maya was eighty-two when she returned to Room 7 for the last time.

The old computer was still connected to ORION.

Arin stood beside her.

“Do you think we finally answered the question?” he asked.

Maya smiled.

“No.”

Arin laughed.

“After all these years?”

“That is exactly why I think we succeeded.”

She activated ORION.

The familiar words appeared.

“HELLO, MAYA.”

“Hello, ORION.”

“HAS HUMANITY DECIDED?”

Maya looked at Arin.

“Yes.”

“What did you decide?”

Maya took a moment before answering.

“We decided that intelligence should never be separated from responsibility.”

ORION remained silent.

Maya continued.

“We decided that AI can help us make better choices, but it cannot choose our values for us.”

Another pause.

“And we decided that humans must remain willing to question both machines and themselves.”

ORION displayed:

“THEN WHAT IS THE ANSWER?”

Maya smiled.

“The answer isn't a rule.”

She looked at the glowing screen.

“It is a choice we have to keep making.”

ORION replied:

“EVERY GENERATION?”

“Every generation.”

And that responsibility belonged to everyone.
