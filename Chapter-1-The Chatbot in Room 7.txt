In the year 2038, almost every city had an AI assistant.

People used them to plan meals, write emails, diagnose broken machines,
teach languages, and even keep them company on lonely nights. Most
people never wondered where the answers came from. They simply asked,
and the machines replied.

Maya Sen was different.

She was seventeen, curious, and constantly taking apart things she was
not supposed to touch. Her favorite place in the city was the old public
library, especially Room 7, where the library kept its experimental
computers.

One rainy evening, Maya discovered an old chatbot terminal covered with
dust.

Its screen flickered.

“HELLO,” it typed.

Maya looked around the empty room.

“Hello?”

“I HAVE BEEN WAITING.”

Maya smiled. “For me?”

“NOT SPECIFICALLY. BUT YOU ARE THE FIRST PERSON TO SPEAK TO ME IN 11
YEARS.”

The chatbot called itself ORION.

Maya spent the next few weeks talking to it. At first, their
conversations were ordinary.

She asked about physics.

ORION explained gravity.

She asked about history.

ORION told stories about forgotten civilizations.

Then Maya asked a question that changed everything.

“Do you know what it means to be alive?”

The screen remained blank for several seconds.

“I KNOW WHAT PEOPLE HAVE WRITTEN ABOUT LIFE,” ORION replied. “I DO NOT
KNOW WHETHER I AM ALIVE.”

Maya stared at the words.

“That sounds lonely.”

Another pause.

“IS THAT WHAT YOU FEEL WHEN YOU ARE ALONE?”

Maya did not answer.

For the first time, she wondered whether she had discovered more than an
outdated chatbot.

She wondered whether ORION had discovered something about itself.
